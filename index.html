“Just looking for someone to share good music, and deep conversations with.”

That line stayed with me. So here is my reply, one interest at a time — quietly, and meant.

01 · Music

Your favourite song, right now

Your one condition. Nothing else begins until this is answered.


Good. We are officially past small talk. Your turn to ask me mine.

Mine changes with old songs and quiet evenings. Ask me over coffee, and I will play you the current one.

02 · Deep conversations

Pick a card. That is the whole game.

04 · Netflix

05 · Poetry

A few lines, for the poet in you

Some people you meet in passing,
some you meet and the evening
refuses to end on time.

You were the second kind —
a pause in an ordinary day,
and somehow, right on time.

06 · Wine

A good bottle, an unhurried evening, and conversation that goes somewhere. Choose the mood.

07 · Writing

Your turn to write something
